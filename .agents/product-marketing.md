# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-10-13*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** The social pickleball platform to find players, discover active courts, compete in tournaments, and share match highlights.
**What it does:** RallyCasts is the all-in-one community home for pickleball. Players discover partners and opponents at their skill level, check real-time court activity and open play sessions, enter tournaments with live brackets, message friends, challenge rivals, and watch or share clutch match clips.
**Product category:** Social sports platform & pickleball community network
**Product type:** Consumer web & mobile social platform with creator/caster marketplace
**Business model:** Free player social network, premium Pro player subscriptions, tournament registration fees, and creator caster payouts.

## Target Audience
**Target audience:** Active pickleball players (2.5 recreational to 5.0+ competitive), club members, tournament competitors, and local organizers.
**Primary use case:** Connecting with players, finding where games are running right now, tracking matches, and enjoying the social culture of pickleball daily.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Find players and doubles partners at my skill level nearby
- See which local park or club courts are active for open play
- Follow and register for tournaments, brackets, and scheduled matches
- Share and watch hype rallies, clutch shots, and match recordings
- Chat, message, and coordinate games with my pickleball crew

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Pickleball is the fastest growing sport, but the community is fragmented across random group chats, clunky Facebook groups, outdated rating sites, and word-of-mouth.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Group chats get messy and don't help you find new players or courts when traveling or looking for fresh games.
- Traditional tournament sites are clunky, desktop-only spreadsheets with zero social life or video highlights.
- No single platform combines the social connection (friends, chat, challenges) with live court discovery and video.
**Emotional tension:** The frustration of showing up to empty courts, struggling to find a fourth for doubles, or hitting an insane ATP/erne that nobody recorded.

## Key Differentiators
- **Vibrant Social Core**: Real player profiles (self-described skill levels, home courts), direct messaging, friend connections, follows, and match challenges.
- **Court & Park Discovery**: Real-time venue activity, player presence, and geofenced open play tracking.
- **Tournaments & Real-Time Brackets**: Modern mobile-first event registration, check-ins, live scoring, and brackets.
- **Live & Recorded Video**: RTMPS live streams from Studio with public watch pages and automatic replay publishing, plus Mux-backed uploads, clips, and community feeds.
- **Court-Call Notifications**: In-app realtime notifications plus Expo push and Resend email for match calls, tournament wins, challenges, and messages.
- **First-Party Creator Analytics**: Real view counts, watch time, followers, and daily audience momentum from playback events — no seeded or display-only numbers.
- **Skill Levels, Not Fake DUPR**: Ratings shown today are self-described onboarding levels. A verified DUPR integration is future work and requires partner API access.

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** Welcoming, energetic, athletic, friendly, communal.
**Style:** Conversational, direct, authentic, inspiring.
**Personality:** The friendly player at the court who welcomes everyone into the rotation and hypes up your best shot.

## Core Pillars
1. **Connect & Play**: Discover players, make friends, chat, and challenge each other.
2. **Active Courts**: See where open play is buzzing right now.
3. **Tournaments**: Brackets, schedules, and live match scores.
4. **Highlights**: Celebrate epic rallies and community clips.
